**In short**

- The visit now follows the Figma file: How can I help → What are you looking for → Build your profile →
  consent → hold still (3-2-1, flash, All set) → **Skin Tone** → **Fit Analysis** → **Suggestions** →
  **Virtual try-on** → Great / Perfect → Bye. The look renders in the background during Skin Tone and Fit,
  and a shortcut jumps straight to it.
- Edit a look by saying what to change ("keep the rest… change the shoes"), one change at a time; earlier
  sets stay reachable.
- **Still here?** When the camera loses the visitor for 10 s, the kiosk asks for 30 s before the visit
  ends (about 40 s in all). Coming back into view, or pressing Still here, keeps the visit.
- **Your results:** the profile icon shows a QR to a temporary page of the visit (undertone, colours,
  fit notes, and a look only if the visitor took it home). Same rules as a kept look.
- **Hand gestures:** only the main hand counts; swipe between outfits on Suggestions; pinch and hold the
  bin for 1.5 s to leave.
- **Shop demo** (cart, filter, checkout, product prices) is off unless `VITE_SHOP_DEMO=true`, because
  its prices and products are placeholders.
- `/privacy` (the consent screen's QR) says what the camera, photo, voice and results do, and for how long.
- Tests: backend 121, sidecar 167, UI 50, all passing.
